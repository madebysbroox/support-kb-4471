The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads dates month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so any day of 12 or less comes out swapped: 7 April becomes 4 July. This is what #4471 says it is, and it is most of the reports. Ticket: #1.

hint: booking-v3’s form hints MM/DD/YYYY and reads the date day first. People type what the hint tells them, and the form reads it the tenant’s way. It looks like the parser bug, but it only comes in through the form, only from tenants on booking-v3, and the parser fix won’t touch it. The desk’s own cause table already marks it “not this ticket — no ticket of its own”. Ticket: #2.

shift: dates move one day, not a swap. Tuesday shows as Monday; the same meeting lands on different days in Lisbon and Toronto; an invite arrives before the booking was made. That is a date going through a time zone and losing a day. The desk read these as one-day moves (desk-notes.json) but left them filed under the parser. Ticket: #3.

Why they were merged

Decision 0001 merges on subject line, and the desk merged further by hand: #4471 holds a dozen different subjects. The subject says the date is wrong; it can’t say why. Intake’s tag doesn’t help either: it is a guess from the reporter’s browser, and it disagrees with the problem on many reports (see the note under the table).

For the desk

  • Before marking a big ticket known, run its reports through causeOf (triage) and the one-day notes. More than one problem: split it.
  • #4471 is closed, so new one-day reports go to #3. A date swapped on a day-first tenant after 5 October is a new problem: the parser fix should have stopped those.
